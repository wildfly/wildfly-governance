https://github.com/wildfly/wildfly-governance/blob/main/wildfly-commonhaus-org/GOVERNANCE.md[WildFly Commonhaus Organization].

The content here was initially generated using the https://github.com/bstansberry/git-file-adder/blob/main/GitOrganizationGroupIds.java[GitOrganizationGroupIds JBang script].

Subsequent manual edits are either in **bold text**, *italic text* or in ~~strikethrough text~~. Bold is for manually added, strikethrough is for manual deletions. Italics are used to indicate groupIds that are not used for publishing non-jbossorg version artifacts to a remote Maven repository. The italics 'not used' treatment is currently limited to the 'Active GroupId List' section.
